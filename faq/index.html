Frequently asked questions

Everything about features, sharing, health, privacy and pricing. Still stuck? In the app, Settings > Family & sharing > Send feedback, we read every note.

Getting started

Is Cubby free?
Yes. Logging, caregiver sharing, your country's vaccine schedule, growth charts, the health hub and keepsakes are free, and always will be. Pro (coming soon) adds extras. We never paywall logging or sharing.
How do I sign up?
Open the app and continue with Google, about ten seconds, no password, no app store.
Is Cubby an app I download?
It runs in any modern browser. On a phone, tap Share then "Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Log while offline and it syncs automatically once you're back online.
Which devices work?
Any modern phone, tablet or computer browser. Your log syncs across all of them.

Logging & features

What can I track?
Feeds (breast, bottle, solids), sleep with an ongoing-nap timer, nappies, pumping, activities, medicines, temperature, illnesses, growth, vaccines, milestones and photos.
Can I log activities like tummy time, sun time or skin-to-skin?
Yes. Tag and time the good-for-baby moments, walks, tummy time, sun time, skin-to-skin and more, and add a photo to any of them.
Can I backdate or edit an entry?
Yes. Anything can be logged for an earlier time and edited or removed afterwards.
Can I track twins or more than one baby?
Yes. Add as many babies as you like, switch between them, or log for more than one at once.
Are there growth charts?
Yes. Plot weight and height on WHO and CDC percentile curves and watch the trend over time.

Sharing & caregivers

Can both parents and a nanny use the same baby?
Yes. Everything syncs in real time across every caregiver, with who-did-what on each entry and a shared handoff note.
How do I invite someone?
In the app, Settings > Family & sharing, invite by email and pick their relationship (mother, father, grandparent, nanny, and so on).
Can I see who logged what?
Yes. Every entry shows who added it, so you always know whether it was you, your partner or the nanny.
Can I remove a caregiver?
Yes, any time, from Settings > Family & sharing.
Who can see my baby's data?
Only the people you invite. Your log is locked to your family, nobody else can see it.

Health & vaccines

Can I edit the vaccine schedule or change country?
Yes. Every item is editable to match your clinic, and switching country keeps the doses you've already logged.
Does Cubby remind me about vaccines and medicine?
Yes, with gentle in-app reminders and due dates. Push notifications when the app is closed are coming with Pro.
Can I track medicines and doses?
Yes. Set an every-few-hours or daily schedule and Cubby counts down to the next dose, so "did we already give it?" never happens.
Does Cubby replace my doctor?
No. It's informational and helps you keep track. Always follow your doctor, midwife or health visitor.
Will Cubby track pregnancy?
Yes, a week-by-week pregnancy tracker (development, appointments, symptoms and danger-sign guidance) is on the way, so Cubby covers day zero through the first year and beyond.

Photos & keepsakes

What keepsakes can I make?
Turn any photo into an Instagram-ready card (square, portrait or story), plus monthly memory cards, milestones and a birth poster.
Do the photo tools send my pictures anywhere?
No. Auto-enhance and background cutout run entirely on your device, nothing is uploaded for those.
Where are my photos stored?
Privately, in Cubby's own secure backend, visible only to your family.

Privacy, data & account

Is my data private and secure?
Yes. No ads, no third-party trackers, never sold. Your log is locked to the people you invite and talks only to Cubby's own secure backend.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Settings > Data > Export downloads everything as a JSON file.
Can I delete everything?
Yes. Settings > Data > Delete all data permanently erases your household's logs. Consider exporting first.
How do I log out?
Settings > Log out (also on your profile). Your data stays safely synced, just sign back in with Google.

Pricing

What's free versus Pro?
All the essentials are free forever: logging, sharing, vaccine schedule, growth, health hub and keepsakes. Pro adds routines, push reminders, premium keepsakes, HD backup, PDF reports and insights. See the pricing page.
What will Pro cost?
Planned at about $15/month on the annual plan ($19 month to month), with localized pricing. It isn't on sale yet, join the waitlist in the app.
Will you ever charge for logging or sharing?
No. The core, logging and caregiver sharing, stays free.
