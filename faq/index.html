Getting started

Is Cubby free?
Yes. Logging, caregiver sharing, your country's vaccine schedule, growth charts, the health hub, routines and keepsakes are free, and always will be. Cubby Pro (launching August 2026) adds extras like the full keepsake studio, watermark-free shares, push reminders and doctor PDF reports. We never paywall logging or sharing.
How do I sign up?
Open the app and continue with Google, or ask for an email sign-in link: we send a link, you tap it, you're in. About ten seconds either way, no password, no app store. Start free.
Is Cubby an app I download?
It runs in any modern browser. On a phone, install it like an app in two taps so it opens full-screen from its own icon (see the next answer).
How do I install it on my iPhone or Android?
iPhone: open little-cubby.com/app in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add". Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". The bear icon appears on your home screen and Cubby opens like a normal app.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Log while offline and it syncs automatically once you're back online.
Which devices work?
Any modern phone, tablet or computer browser. Your log syncs across all of them.

Logging & your day

What can I track?
Feeds (breast, bottle, solids), sleep with an ongoing-nap timer, nappies, pumping, activities, medicines, temperature, illnesses, growth, vaccines, milestones and photos. See all the features.
What are the notes on my home screen?
Your home screen shows a gentle day surface: a place for short notes about today, a recent photo or two, and a calm recap. Leave a note for everyone (a handoff like "slept well, last bottle 2:10") or keep one private to yourself or to one person. Notes are their own private records, not buried in a feed.
What are routines, and are they free?
Cubby suggests gentle, age-appropriate routines for each baby in the Log tab, like tummy time, a wind-down, or vitamin D. Tap one to log it as a real entry. The routines themselves are free. Smart adaptive routines and push reminders are part of Pro.
Can I log activities like tummy time, sun time or skin-to-skin?
Yes. Tag and time the good-for-baby moments, walks, tummy time, sun time, skin-to-skin and more, and add a photo to any of them.
Can I backdate or edit an entry?
Yes. Anything can be logged for an earlier time and edited or removed afterwards.
Can I track twins or more than one baby?
Yes. Add as many babies as you like, switch between them, or log for more than one at once.
Are there growth charts?
Yes. Plot weight and height on WHO and CDC percentile curves and watch the trend over time.

Sharing & your care circle

Can both parents and a nanny use the same baby?
Yes. Everything syncs in real time across every caregiver, with who-did-what on each entry. Invite or remove people any time.
How do I invite someone?
In the app, Settings > Family & sharing, invite by email and pick their relationship (mother, father, grandparent, nanny, and so on).
Can I see who logged what?
Yes. Every entry shows who added it, so you always know whether it was you, your partner or the nanny.
Does everyone see everything, or can I share by parts?
Your baby's day-to-day log is shared with the caregivers you invite, so the whole circle stays in sync. The sensitive things are different: a mother's own health and her pregnancy journey are private by default and shared only with the specific people she chooses, item by item. More on what we stand for.
Can I remove a caregiver?
Yes, any time, from Settings > Family & sharing.

Pregnancy

Does Cubby track pregnancy?
Yes. Cubby walks the whole journey with you, from trying and a positive test through week-by-week development, your antenatal schedule, gentle health trackers, a kick counter and contraction timer, and a Moments album for your scans. On the big day it becomes your baby tracker, same app, same story. See the pregnancy guide.
Is my pregnancy private?
Yes, private by default. Your pregnancy journey lives in your own space, not the shared family log, and it's shared only with the people you explicitly choose. Your health details like weight, blood pressure and glucose stay mother-owned, and your mood notes can never be shared with anyone. Why privacy is the point.
Does Cubby handle pregnancy loss gently?
Yes, as gently as we can. You're always in control of your own journey and what you keep or remove. Cubby is informational and never a substitute for your midwife or doctor; if something doesn't feel right, contact them straight away.

Privacy & who owns your data

Who owns my data?
You do. You're the source of truth for your family's record. You decide who is invited, what the sensitive parts share, and you can export or delete everything yourself, anytime. What we stand for.
Is my data private and secure?
Yes. No ads, no third-party trackers, never sold. Your log is locked to the people you invite, enforced by server-side security rules, and your data is encrypted in transit and at rest (it lives in Google Cloud Firestore). The site also ships strict browser security headers (HSTS, content-security policy, no framing). You can export or delete everything yourself in Settings, anytime.
Who can see my baby's data?
Only the people you invite. Your log is locked to your family, nobody else can see it, and the most sensitive parts, a mother's health and the pregnancy journey, are shared only with whom she chooses.
Is the email sign-in link secure?
Yes. The link is single-use and expires soon after we send it, and the request is rate-limited to stop abuse. If a link expires or you don't see it, just ask for a new one.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Settings > Data > Export downloads everything as a JSON file.
Can I delete everything?
Yes. Settings > Data > Delete all data permanently erases your household's logs. Only an owner can do this, and it's worth exporting first.
How do I log out?
Settings > Log out (also on your profile). Your data stays safely synced, just sign back in.

Health & vaccines

Can I edit the vaccine schedule or change country?
Yes. Every item is editable to match your clinic, and switching country keeps the doses you've already logged.
Does Cubby remind me about vaccines and medicine?
Yes, with gentle in-app reminders and due dates. Push notifications when the app is closed are coming with Pro.
Can I track medicines and doses?
Yes. Set an every-few-hours or daily schedule and Cubby counts down to the next dose, so "did we already give it?" never happens.
Does Cubby replace my doctor?
No. It's informational and helps you keep track. Always follow your doctor, midwife or health visitor and your country's official guidance.

Photos & keepsakes

What keepsakes can I make?
Turn any photo into an Instagram-ready card (square, portrait or story), plus monthly memory cards, milestones and a birth poster.
Do the photo tools send my pictures anywhere?
No. Auto-enhance and background cutout run entirely on your device, nothing is uploaded for those.
Where are my photos stored?
Privately, in Cubby's own secure backend, visible only to your family.

Will you ever charge for logging or sharing?
No. The core, logging and caregiver sharing, stays free.
