Frequently asked questions

Is Cubby free?
Yes. Logging, caregiver sharing, your country's vaccine schedule, growth charts and keepsakes are free, and always will be. Cubby Pro (coming soon) adds extras like routines, push reminders and premium keepsakes. We'll never paywall logging or sharing.
Which countries' vaccine schedules does Cubby support?
The UK (NHS), US (CDC), UAE (MOHAP) and Germany (STIKO) today, chosen at setup, with more to come. Every item is editable to match your clinic.
Can both parents and a nanny use the same baby?
Yes. Everything syncs in real time across every caregiver, with shared handoff notes and who-did-what on each entry.
Is my baby's data private?
Yes. No ads, no third-party trackers, and we never sell your data. Your log is locked to your family and talks only to Cubby's own secure backend.
Is Cubby an app I download?
It runs in any browser, no app store needed. On a phone, tap Share then "Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app, with offline support.
Does Cubby replace my doctor?
No. Cubby is informational and helps you keep track. Always follow your doctor, health visitor or clinic, and your country's official guidance.
How do I share feedback?
In the app, go to Settings → Family & sharing → Send feedback. We read every note.
