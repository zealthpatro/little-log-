Getting started & installing

Is Cubby an app I download from the App Store?
No. Cubby runs in any modern browser and installs to your home screen in two taps, so it opens full-screen like a normal app, with no app store and nothing to update.
How do I install it on my iPhone or Android?
iPhone: open little-cubby.com/app in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then "Install app". The bear icon appears and Cubby opens like a normal app.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Log while offline and it syncs automatically once you're back online.
Which devices and browsers work?
Any modern phone, tablet or computer browser. Your log syncs across all of them.
Do I need to create a password?
No. Continue with Google, or ask for a one-tap email sign-in link. About ten seconds either way, and no password to remember. Start free.
How long does setup take?
Under a minute: choose expecting or baby, add a name and a date, and you're in.
Is there a tutorial, or do I just start?
You just start. The home screen guides you, and you can add details as you go.

Account, sign-in & security

Is the email sign-in link secure?
Yes. The link is single-use, expires soon after we send it, and the request is rate-limited. If a link expires, just ask for a new one.
Can I use both Google and email sign-in?
Yes. Either way signs you into the same account.
How do I log out?
Settings > Log out (also on your profile). Your data stays safely synced, just sign back in.
What happens if I switch phones?
Just sign in on the new device and everything syncs from the cloud. There's nothing to transfer.
Can two people be signed in at once?
Yes. Every caregiver signs in on their own device and sees the same live log.
What if I lose access to my email?
Reach us via Settings > Family & sharing > Send feedback, and we'll help you recover access.
Is there two-factor sign-in?
Google sign-in carries your Google account security, and the email link is single-use and time-limited.

Logging your day — feeds, sleep, nappies, pumping

What can I track?
Feeds (breast, bottle, solids), sleep with an ongoing-nap timer, nappies, pumping, activities, medicines, temperature, illnesses, growth, vaccines, milestones and photos. See all the features.
How fast is logging a feed or nap?
Two taps. It's designed for one thumb, half-asleep, at 3am.
Does an ongoing nap show on everyone's phone?
Yes. A live timer appears for every caregiver the moment it starts.
Can I backdate or edit an entry?
Yes. Anything can be logged for an earlier time and edited or removed afterwards.
Can I track pumping sessions?
Yes. Pumping is a first-class log type alongside feeds and sleep.
Can I log breast and bottle differently?
Yes. Choose breast, bottle or solids, each with the right fields.
Can I add a note or photo to any entry?
Yes. Add a note to any log, and a photo to feeds, solids and activities.
What are the notes on my home screen?
A gentle "today" surface for short handoff notes, shared with everyone or kept private to you or to one person.
Can I see the whole day at a glance?
Yes. The home recap and the Log tab show feeds, naps, nappies and more for the day.
Can I delete an entry I added by mistake?
Yes. Remove any entry, and deletions are recoverable from "Recently deleted".
Will it remind me of the last feed or nap time?
Yes. The home cards show last feed, last sleep and last nappy at a glance.

Solids, food & mealtimes

Can I log solids with the foods offered?
Yes. Tag foods such as banana, oats or rice and note how much went down.
Can I photograph the plate before and after?
Yes. Add photos to a meal so you and the nanny can see what was offered and eaten.
Can I track allergies and new foods?
Yes. Record allergies and diet notes in the Health hub, and they show on the baby's profile.
Can I localise food tags like idli or ragi?
You can tag any food you type. Richer regional food libraries are on the roadmap.
Is there a nutrition view over time?
A nutrition view that turns meal logs into trends is a Cubby Pro feature, launching August 2026.
Can both parents and the nanny see what was eaten?
Yes. Meals sync live to the whole care circle, with who logged them.

Sharing & your care circle

Can both parents and a nanny use the same baby?
Yes. Everything syncs in real time across every caregiver, with who-did-what on each entry. Invite or remove people any time.
How do I invite someone?
In the app, Settings > Family & sharing, invite by email and pick their relationship such as mother, father, grandparent or nanny.
Is sharing with family free?
Yes. Unlimited family and caregivers, free forever. We never paywall sharing.
Can I see who logged what?
Yes. Every entry shows who added it.
What's the handoff note?
A shared note for whoever's on duty, like "slept well, last bottle 2:10", so nothing lives in one person's head.
Can I get a "while you were away" recap?
Yes. See a tidy recap of feeds, naps and meals logged by the nanny while you were at work.
Can grandparents or daycare join too?
Yes. Invite anyone who helps care for your baby, with the role you choose.
What happens when I remove a caregiver?
Any time, and only an owner can do it, from Settings > Family & sharing. Their access ends immediately, enforced by server-side rules, so they can no longer open your log on any device and they keep nothing of your baby's data. Everything they logged while caring for your baby stays in the story, still credited to them by name, so the history is never broken, and any pending invite is cancelled. You can re-invite them later.
What can a caregiver see, and what can't they?
A caregiver sees the shared baby log in real time and can add entries and edit their own. They cannot see a mother's private health or her pregnancy unless she shares those item by item, and only an owner can manage who's in the circle, edit other people's entries, or delete the account.
Can a caregiver keep private notes?
Yes. Notes can be shared with everyone or kept private to you or to one person.
Does everyone see everything, or can I share by parts?
The baby's day-to-day log is shared with your circle, while a mother's own health and pregnancy are private by default and shared item by item. More on what we stand for.

More than one child & switching

Can I track twins or more than one baby?
Yes. Add as many as you like, switch between them, or log for more than one at once.
How do I switch between children?
Tap the name at the top to open Switch, then pick a child or your pregnancy.
Can a pregnancy and a baby live in the same account?
Yes. Your pregnancy and every child coexist in one private family account.
Can I log for two babies at once?
Yes. Choose "Both" when logging shared moments like a feed time.
What happens with a second pregnancy?
Add it to the same account. Nothing restarts, and each child keeps their own story.

Vaccines & country schedules

Which countries' vaccine schedules are supported?
The UK (NHS), US (CDC), Germany (STIKO), India (IAP/ACVIP), the UAE, Canada, Australia, Ireland, Singapore and more, plus a WHO-aligned plan. Due dates come from your baby's birthday. See the baby features.
Can I edit the schedule to match my clinic?
Yes. Every item is editable.
What if I move country?
Switch the schedule and the doses you've already logged carry over.
How are vaccine due dates calculated?
From your baby's date of birth, against the official schedule for the country you pick.
What if my baby is older and behind?
Cubby shows a calm catch-up view with confirm-with-your-clinic guidance, never a wall of "overdue".
Can I mark a dose as already given?
Yes. Tick off past doses so the schedule reflects reality.
Does it remind me when a vaccine is due?
Yes, with due dates and gentle reminders, and you can turn on a push for a confirmed missed vaccine.
What does each vaccine protect against?
Each item links to plain-language guidance with cited official sources.
Can I keep a record to show the doctor?
Yes. Your logged doses form a record you can review at appointments.
Is there an India vaccine schedule?
Yes. Cubby supports the India (IAP/ACVIP) childhood schedule, editable to your paediatrician.

Medicine, health & the doctor

Can I track medicines and doses?
Yes. Set an every-few-hours or daily schedule and Cubby counts down to the next dose, so "did we already give it?" never happens.
Does the countdown work across caregivers?
Yes. Both parents and the nanny see the same next-dose timer.
Is there fever or temperature guidance?
Yes. Log temperature and see gentle, informational guidance, never a diagnosis.
Can I record illnesses and symptoms?
Yes. The Illness section keeps symptoms so they're not "a memory you wish you'd mentioned".
Can I store my doctor's details?
Yes. The Care team keeps your doctor and a visit log in one place.
Is there a doctor-visit summary?
Yes, a one-tap summary today. A polished doctor PDF report comes with Pro (August 2026).
Where do allergies live?
In the Health hub, shareable with anyone caring for the baby.
Does Cubby replace my doctor?
No. It's informational. Always follow your doctor, midwife or health visitor and your country's official guidance.
Is the medical content reviewed?
Schedules and thresholds summarise official guidance such as the NHS, CDC, WHO and STIKO, with sources cited and linked.

Growth & development

Are there growth charts?
Yes. Plot weight and height on WHO and CDC percentile curves and watch the trend over time.
Can I see the trend, not just one number?
Yes. The chart shows the curve over time with a plain-language read, like "around the 50th centile".
Are there boy and girl charts, in kg or lb?
Yes. Choose the right reference and your preferred units.
Can I track head circumference?
Weight and height are charted today. Head circumference isn't charted yet.
Are there developmental milestones?
Yes. Gentle, age-aware nudges like "around now, lots of babies are working on this", never a pass or fail.
Can I share the growth picture with my doctor?
Yes. Show the chart at checkups.

Reminders & notifications

Does Cubby remind me about vaccines and medicine?
Yes. Turn on Reminders for gentle nudges about a due medicine, a confirmed missed vaccine, or your turn on a routine.
Are reminders on by default?
No. They're off until you choose to turn them on.
Will it nag me about feeds?
Never. Cubby deliberately doesn't send feed reminders.
Can I set quiet hours?
Yes. By default nothing is sent between 21:00 and 7:00.
Do reminders work when the app is closed?
Yes. Gentle web push for a due medicine or a confirmed missed vaccine works with the app closed, off by default, with quiet hours. Richer always-on push expands with Pro.

Pregnancy — the journey

Does Cubby track pregnancy?
Yes. Week-by-week development, your antenatal schedule, gentle health trackers, a kick counter and contraction timer, and a Moments album, becoming your baby tracker on the big day. See the pregnancy guide.
Can I start before I know my due date?
Yes. Enter the first day of your last period and Cubby works out your weeks.
What does the week view show?
How far along you are, your baby's development and "size of", what you may feel, plus quick health check-ins.
Are antenatal appointments pre-loaded?
Yes. Pick your country, such as the UK (NHS), US (ACOG), Germany, the UAE, India (MoHFW) or a WHO-aligned plan, and Cubby seeds the schedule, editable to your clinic.
What's the Moments album?
Each scan, bump photo or the test itself, saved next to the week and the "size of" comparison, your first album.
Is there a kick counter and contraction timer?
Yes. Count to ten movements, and time contractions with gentle 5-1-1 guidance.
Can I keep a birth plan and hospital bag list?
Yes. Both live in Tools, next to the kick and contraction timers.
Are there urgent warning signs?
Yes. One tap from the home screen, summarising official guidance on when to seek care.
Can my partner follow along?
You choose what to share. A fuller partner view is on the roadmap.
Does it cover when we're trying?
There's a gentle preconception checklist, and an optional rough fertile-window estimate if you add your last period. Cubby isn't a fertility or ovulation tracker.

Pregnancy — health, privacy, loss & birth

Can I track gestational diabetes or glucose?
Yes. Log glucose with common targets in mmol/L or mg/dL.
Can I track blood pressure and supplements?
Yes. Opt-in trackers for blood pressure with pre-eclampsia warning signs, supplements, nausea and hydration.
Why are the health trackers opt-in?
So the app stays calm. Turn on only what's relevant to you in "Manage health trackers".
Is my pregnancy private?
Yes. It lives in your own space, not the shared family log, and is shared only with people you explicitly choose. Why privacy is the point.
Can my mood notes ever be shared?
No. Mood and mental-health notes can never be shared with anyone. It's built into the lock, not a setting.
Does Cubby handle pregnancy loss gently?
Yes, as gently as we can. You're always in control of what you keep or remove. If something doesn't feel right, contact your midwife or doctor straight away.
What happens when the baby arrives?
Tap "Baby has arrived" and your pregnancy becomes the baby's profile. The country and details carry over, and scans are kept as the first chapter.
Is the pregnancy kept after birth?
Yes. Nothing is lost. The pregnancy and its Moments stay as history.

Privacy & who owns your data

Who owns my data, and can I delete it?
You do. Export it as a JSON file or permanently delete it. Because the log belongs to the whole household, the big actions, a full export or deletion, ask both guardians to agree first when there are two. What we stand for.
Do you show ads or sell my data?
Never. No ads, no third-party trackers, never sold.
Is my data encrypted?
Yes. Encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in Google Cloud Firestore, with server-side security rules locking your log to invited people only.
Who can see my baby's data?
Only the people you invite, and the most sensitive parts, a mother's health and the pregnancy, only whom she chooses.
What does "item-by-item" sharing mean?
You choose, part by part, who in your circle can see each piece of your health, not all-or-nothing.
Can I recover something I deleted by accident?
Yes. A deleted entry moves to "Recently deleted" and can be restored, and the original logger and time stay on the record. Full account deletion is permanent, and with two guardians it needs both to agree.
Where are my photos stored?
Privately in Cubby's secure backend, visible only to your family. On-device enhance uploads nothing.
Is Cubby GDPR-friendly?
You can request export or permanent deletion of your data, and with two guardians these are confirmed by both. Data minimisation and user control are built in: no ads, no third-party trackers, and nothing sold.

Printed keepsakes are on the roadmap. Today's cards are beautiful digital shares.
