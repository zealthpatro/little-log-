Questions

What vaccines are given at at birth in Saudi Arabia?
At at birth the Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Health schedule includes Hepatitis B (1st dose).
How many vaccination visits are in the Saudi Arabia baby schedule?
The schedule for Saudi Arabia has 9 vaccination visits from birth through 4 to 6 years. Exact ages can vary slightly, so your doctor confirms each visit.
Can both parents and our caregiver keep track together?
