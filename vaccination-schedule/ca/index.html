Canada Baby Vaccine Schedule (2026) (NACI): Immunization Chart

Every vaccine Canada's National Advisory Committee on Immunization recommends for babies, from birth through 4 to 6 years, age by age.

Last reviewed 24 June 2026 · Summarised from the official Government of Canada immunization schedules.

Vaccines and exact timing vary by province and territory. This is the nationally recommended infant schedule; check your province or territory's own schedule for the doses and brands funded where you live.

Dates are a guide based on the recommended schedule. Your doctor confirms the exact visits.

Questions

What vaccines are given at at birth in Canada?
At at birth the Canada's National Advisory Committee on Immunization schedule includes Hepatitis B (HB) (1st dose).
How many vaccination visits are in the Canada baby schedule?
The schedule for Canada has 7 vaccination visits from birth through 4 to 6 years. Exact ages can vary slightly, so your doctor confirms each visit.
Can both parents and our caregiver keep track together?
