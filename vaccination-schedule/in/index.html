India Baby Vaccine Schedule (2026) (IAP): Immunisation Chart

Every vaccine the Indian Academy of Pediatrics recommends for babies, from birth through 4 to 6 years, age by age.

Last reviewed 24 June 2026 · Summarised from the official IAP / ACVIP immunisation schedule.

This is the IAP/ACVIP recommended immunisation schedule used by many paediatricians in India. It is broader than the government Universal Immunization Programme (UIP), which provides a core set of vaccines free of charge. Ask your paediatrician which schedule is right for your baby.

Dates are a guide based on the recommended schedule. Your doctor confirms the exact visits.

Questions

What vaccines are given at birth in India?
At birth the Indian Academy of Pediatrics schedule includes BCG, Hepatitis B (birth dose / 1st dose), OPV (0 / birth dose).
How many vaccination visits are in the India baby schedule?
The schedule for India has 11 vaccination visits from birth through 4 to 6 years. Exact ages can vary slightly, so your doctor confirms each visit.
