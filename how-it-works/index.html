Your first five minutes

Six small steps. Most people are logging before they've finished their cup of tea.

1 · Start in about ten seconds
Open Cubby in any browser and continue with Google, or ask for a one-tap email sign-in link. No password to invent, no app store, nothing to update. You're in before you've finished reading this line.
2 · Add your baby, or your pregnancy
Choose baby or expecting, add a name and a date, and that's the whole setup. Expecting? Enter your due date, or the first day of your last period and Cubby works out your weeks. A pregnancy and every child can share one account.
3 · Log in two taps, one thumb
Tap once to start a feed or nap, once to finish. Feeds, sleep, nappies and pumping, all designed for one thumb, half-asleep, at 3am. Forgot to log in the moment? Backdate it in seconds.
4 · Invite your circle
Bring in your partner, grandparents and the nanny by email, and pick how each one helps. Every entry shows who logged it, so you always know whether that last bottle was you or daycare. Sharing is free, with no limit, ever.
5 · It's in sync everywhere, instantly
An ongoing nap shows a live timer on every phone. Away at work? See feeds, naps and meals the moment the nanny logs them, then open a tidy while-you-were-away recap of the whole day.
6 · It grows with you, bump to big kid
One account, no restarts. When your baby arrives, your pregnancy becomes their profile and your scans stay as the first chapter. Feeds give way to first foods, first steps, big-school mornings. The app is already home.

Bring in the people who love your bump

Cubby has a little joy built in. Family games let you share a Boy-or-girl guess and a due-date pool with friends and family.

A guess, just for the joy of it
Share a link for a Boy-or-girl guess or a due-date pool. Your people open it, add a nickname and their guess, with no account and nothing tracked.
You reveal, when you're ready
Only you can reveal the answer, on your day. It's always optional, and a guess is never anyone's medical answer. Built with care, so a tender journey stays tender.

What you open to, every day

Cubby's home is a warm "Fridge": the calm, time-aware first screen that holds your day.

A home that knows the hour
"Good morning, Papa Bear" by day, "still up? the 3am club" at night. A photo carousel of your little one, the last feed, sleep and nappy at a glance, and a private handoff note for whoever's on duty.
Your pregnancy stays yours
The fact you're expecting lives in your own space, never the shared family log, and reaches someone only when you choose. A mother's own health is shared item by item, and mood notes can't be shared at all, by design.

Your log is locked to the people you invite by server-side rules. No ads, no third-party trackers, nothing sold. Why privacy is the whole point.
