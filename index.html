You walk into the six-week check with it already written down.

One tap lays out the last seven days in the order a doctor asks for them: feeds and how they were given, sleep, wet and dirty nappies, temperatures, growth, medicines and allergies. The averages only count the days Cubby actually watched, so a five-day-old is never quietly averaged over a fortnight.

You know whether the 2am dose was already given.

Tap to log a dose when one is already there inside that medicine's own gap, and Cubby simply tells you: who logged it, at what time, and how long ago. Then it lets you decide, with no warning and no telling off.
