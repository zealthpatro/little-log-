A promise, built into the product

Your family's life is nobody's business but yours.

Not written on a page, enforced in how the app is built.

✕
We will never sell or rent your family's data.
Not to advertisers, not to brokers, not "anonymised", not ever. Cubby earns from Cubby Pro, never from you being the product.
✕
No ads. No third-party trackers.
Encrypted in transit and at rest, locked to the people you invite by server-side rules. A mother's health and mood stay hers: mood notes can never be shared, with anyone.
✕
