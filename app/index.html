What Cubby does

  • One-thumb logging for feeds, sleep, nappies and pumping, even at 3am.
  • Your whole care circle sees the same log, live, with who-did-what attribution.
  • Vaccine schedule with due dates and reminders so you never miss a dose.
  • Growth charts on WHO and CDC percentile curves.
  • Health hub: medicine reminders, gentle fever guidance and a one-tap doctor summary.
  • Keepsakes: monthly memory cards, milestones and a birth poster from your real moments.
  • Private by design: no ads, no third-party trackers, we never sell your data.
