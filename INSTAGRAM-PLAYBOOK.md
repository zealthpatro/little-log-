> Companion to COMMUNITY-PLAYBOOK.md. That one is the Reddit/forum trust-grind; this is the
> owned Instagram channel. Built June 2026, locked after a full Product Board + adversarial QA pass.
> Everything here serves CUBBY-EXPERIENCE-CHARTER.md. If a post fails the Anxiety Test, it does not ship.

## The one idea (read this first)

A faceless calm account does not go viral. **The share card does.** Cubby already ships a
`made with Cubby · little-cubby.com` footer on every free keepsake export (Then & Now, first-smile,
monthly cards). When a mother makes a beautiful free keepsake and shares it to her story for her own
reasons, that footer rides along to her whole circle. That is the entire growth engine, white-hat by
construction, zero ad spend. The feed's only job is to teach that the cards exist and to bank trust.
Make the free card lovely. Never enlarge the footer.

So we do not optimise posts for reach. We optimise for the **save** (something she returns to at 3am)
and the **send** (something she texts to one person). Those are the signals that actually grow a warm,
retained cohort, which is the stage goal (billing is live Oct 2026; we want a retained, Pro-waitlisted
crowd, not a spike of signups who churn).

## Content pillars (and the white space they own)

Every competitor (Huckleberry, BabyCenter, Flo) grows by creating worry their content then solves.
Cubby is the only account whose job is to make the scroll quieter. That is a moat they structurally
cannot copy.

1. **Calm in the chaos** — reassurance / permission cards ("rough nights happen", "a gap is a chapter").
   The follow-driver and the most save-and-send-able pillar. Speaks to the feeling, never the data act.
2. **The shared log** — the caregiver-sync wedge (one live log, every entry shows who logged it). The
   strongest, least-crowded angle. Frame as making the invisible mental load visible, never as a way to
   check up on anyone, and never assume she has a partner.
3. **Your data is yours** — the privacy wedge, post-Flo/post-Roe trust. Copy-locked (see below).
4. **Know-how** — the article library turned into save-worthy explainers, calm not clinical.
5. **Keepsake** — the growth engine (Then & Now, first-smile, monthly). Free-first, fictional baby,
   whisper footer. Also seeds the Pro keepsake-studio desire.

## The keepsake growth engine

- The free keepsake export carries the `made with Cubby · little-cubby.com` footer. That is the loop.
- Every marketing example uses a FICTIONAL baby (illustrated bear), never a real user's export.
- The footer is sacred: never enlarge, brighten, duplicate, or move it. One small quiet line.
- Verified gating: free users CAN make footer-carrying keepsakes (Then & Now via a few free "tastes",
  monthly/share cards free-with-footer). Pro (Oct 2026) = watermark-free + unlimited + full studio.
- Honesty rule in captions: "the everyday **logging** is free, always" (not "everyday stuff"), and never
  imply unlimited-free keepsakes.

### In-app footer (shipped June 2026)

The real in-app footer was upgraded from the OS bear emoji to a custom-drawn bear (helper
`drawCubbyFooter` in app/index.html, used by composeShareCard, composeMemoryCard, drawThenNow, and the
poster). Reason: the emoji renders differently on every device; a drawn bear in the footer's own colour
looks identical everywhere and unmistakably ours, on every keepsake that travels.

## The honest purchase-intent ladder

"Purchase intent early" means seed genuine desire and a warm waitlist, NOT a hard sell. Pro billing is
Oct 2026. Seed Pro ONLY at the activated stage, and frame it as a gift arriving, never a gate closing.

1. **Follower** — calm static earns the follow by lowering her heart rate. No Pro mention. Measure save + follow.
2. **Article reader** — caption links to one of 186 articles (carries app + newsletter). First-party UTM stamps it.
3. **Free app user** — get families onto free Cubby early. Sell nothing. Bank warmth.
4. **Activated / PQL** — first doctor summary generated OR first watermarked keepsake exported. The only
   honest moment to seed Pro desire (she has felt the value).
5. **Warm waitlist** — soft line: "the keepsake studio and doctor report arrive Oct 2026, want a note when
   they do? no card, no rush." Rename the ask "Register for Pro" to **"get told when it's ready"**.
6. **Day-one buyer (Oct 2026)** — convert only the retained, activated, multi-caregiver cohort.

**Pro disclosure (permanent, on any Pro-adjacent post):** "the everyday logging is free, always" +
"keepsake studio + doctor report arrive Oct 2026, no card, no charge until then." Cap Pro-flavoured
posts at ~1 in 4. No countdown, no scarcity, no "limited spots".

## The card-kit (6 reusable layouts)

4:5 (1080x1350) default, same ratio across a carousel. Fraunces display + Nunito body. Warm-den palette:
cream `#F4EEE3`, ink `#2C2521`, ink-soft `#6E635B`, surface `#FFFFFF`, line `#E7DECF`. Category colours
label DATA ONLY. Pump rose `#C97FA0` on CTA pills ONLY. Bear in one place. Keep art and text out of the
bottom 12%. Build the 4:5 master in Figma first, then resize variants.

1. **Reassurance / being-seen card** — single calm line, speaks to the feeling. The follow-driver.
2. **Carousel cover** — calm specific hook, pillar kicker, ~80% of the swipe decision.
3. **Carousel inner slide** — one gentle idea; final slide carries the single soft CTA.
4. **App-demo phone mock** — seeded demo household only, 390px light theme, real app icons.
5. **Tip / checklist card** — save-worthy reference, no urgency, no red-flag scare decoration.
6. **Keepsake card** — Then & Now / first-smile / monthly, fictional baby, whisper footer (never enlarged).

### The Anxiety-Test gate (pin this; run before every export)

> Does this card relieve an anxiety she already has, or manufacture one to sell the cure? If it
> manufactures, do not post. Then confirm: savable AND sendable, calm in / calm out, sentence case, no
> em-dashes, humanized numbers, no assumed partner / second baby / "should", and if any locked/Pro
> artifact is shown the two disclosure lines are present. One no = it does not ship.

### Banned phrases (baked into the template)

`end-to-end` · `zero-knowledge` · `we can't see your data` · `fully private` · `encrypted` · `anonymous`
· `behind` / `are you behind` · `on track` · `enough` (as in "is she eating enough") · `don't miss` ·
`never miss a feed` · `every mom` · `the symptom` · any percentile-as-grade · any number-as-target (a
streak, "47 naps", "8 feeds a day") · `limited spots` · `before it's too late` · `bounce back` ·
`Register for Pro` (use "get told when it's ready") · `last feed 2:10` or any claim asserting the state
of HER data · user-count / "thousands of parents" · `tag a tired mom` (engagement-bait) · `should`
(implying she failed) · fear words (`must` / `warning` / `wrong` / `mistake`).

## Verified privacy-claim scope (checked against deployed firestore.rules, June 2026)

- **Safe to claim (server-enforced):** mother-owned health, item by item, who sees what
  (`mhealth/{owner}/cat/{category}` sharedWith); mood notes can never be shared (mood category is
  owner-only, rules forbid any sharedWith); notes private-to-one or circle, locked at create; pregnancy
  journey owner-owned; outsiders blocked; Pro entitlement set only by the billing Worker.
- **Do NOT claim as a guarantee:** dual-guardian consent for export/delete is client-side only, not
  rules-enforced. Email is visible to circle members. Never imply otherwise.
- **Never:** end-to-end / zero-knowledge / "we can't see your data". It is access control + never-sold,
  not encryption.
- Belt-and-suspenders before the privacy card goes on a high-reach post: run the PRIVACY-MAX-1.0
  emulator cross-account denial test (still a P0).

## Metrics and ops

**North-star (not likes, not reach):** saves per post · sends / DM-forwards · profile + link taps ·
PQL activation (first doctor summary or first keepsake export) · Pro-waitlist 30-day retention ·
multi-caregiver household formation · watermarked keepsake exports per active household (the literal
viral coefficient). **Anti-metric tripwire:** if a fear/urgency post spikes reach, log it as a FAILURE,
not a win.

**UTM (first-party, consistent with COMMUNITY-PLAYBOOK + tools/analytics.js):**
`?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=<surface>&utm_campaign=<topic>&utm_content=<angle>`. `utm_medium` =
`profile` (bio link), `story` (Stories link-sticker), `reel` (optional weekly reel). Angles:
`caregiver-sync | privacy | doctor-summary | calm | keepsake`. Reserve `utm_medium=community` for
Reddit/forums so Instagram never mixes into the community report. No Meta/IG pixel, ever.

**Batching:** a month of static (12 posts) in 1-2 sittings from the card-kit. Sitting 1 = write (hook +
slides + caption + alt text, run the gate). Sitting 2 = produce in the templates + load into Business
Suite native scheduler. Keep a tiny content log (date, pillar, topic, utm_campaign/content, + blank
columns for saves/sends/taps).

**Stories:** light and dependable, 2-3 frames a few times a week. Reshared slide, a quote card, or a
one-tap poll ("rough night or smooth night?"). No streak guilt. A quiet week is allowed.

**Replies:** warm, brief, second person, the 9:1 spirit. Lead with the answer or reassurance, never the
link. Run the Anxiety Test on every reply. Never overclaim to win a comment.

**Review:** monthly, ~45-60 min. Rank posts by saves-per-reach and sends, not likes. Lean next month
toward what earned saves and sends. Check waitlist 30-day retention and the IG funnel in
`node tools/analytics.js`. Flag any post that drifted toward fear/overclaim and tighten the template.

## Kill-list (rejected by the Red Team; do not ship)

- Any "we can't see your data" / E2E / zero-knowledge privacy claim. Copy-lock the true wording.
- The full mother-owned-circle model as a proven guarantee before the emulator cross-account test passes
  (the mood-lock + item-by-item sharing ARE enforced; do not stretch beyond them).
- The "she's been fussy?" doctor framing. Credit her; never shame.
- Any fear / urgency / comparison hook. Categorical Anxiety-Test failure regardless of performance.
- The public "47 naps" monthly-stats count. A count implies a target.
- Firing the `?ref=` referral reward before Oct 2026.
- Any Meta/IG pixel or bio-link analytics SDK.
- Bare "Register for Pro" with no disclosure; any fake scarcity / countdown / user-count / fake testimonial.
- Enlarging the keepsake footer for reach.
- Posting any real baby's card, name, or photo. Fictional baby + seeded demo household, always.
- "Never miss a feed" reminder framing. Cubby holds it so she does not have to; never a vigilance prompt.
- Engagement-bait ("tag a tired mom", worry-rehearsal comment prompts) to hit the 3x/week quota.
