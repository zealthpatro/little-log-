Frequently asked questions

What is Cubby?

Cubby is a baby care companion that lets parents and caregivers track feeds, naps, nappies, medicines and vaccines in a shared real-time log. It covers pregnancy through the baby's first years and includes 420+ articles on baby health, feeding, sleep and development.

How is Cubby different from other baby tracking apps?

Cubby focuses on the coordination problem: keeping every caregiver — partner, grandparent, nanny — in sync without a group chat. It also covers UAE, India, UK and Australian vaccine schedules, has no ads or third-party trackers, and works in any browser without an app store download.

Is Cubby free?

Yes. Core tracking and the full article library are free. A Pro tier with keepsakes and doctor visit PDFs is coming soon.

Which countries does Cubby cover?

Cubby has content for families in the UAE, India, UK, Australia, USA, Germany, Japan and Italy — including country-specific vaccine schedules, antenatal care guides, and parental rights articles.

Does Cubby have ads or sell data?

No. Cubby has no ads and no third-party analytics pixels. Your family's health data stays in Cubby's own infrastructure and is never sold or shared.
