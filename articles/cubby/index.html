Frequently asked questions

What is Cubby?

Cubby is a baby care companion that lets parents and caregivers track feeds, naps, nappies, medicines and vaccines in a real-time shared log. It covers pregnancy through the first years and includes 420+ articles for families in the UAE, India, UK, Australia and the US.

Who is Cubby for?

Any family who wants to coordinate care across more than one person: partners, grandparents, nannies, helpers. Also single parents who need a reliable record, and expat families navigating multiple healthcare systems.

Is Cubby free?

Yes. Core tracking and the full article library are free. A Pro tier with keepsakes and doctor-visit PDF export is coming soon.

Does Cubby need to be downloaded from an app store?

No. Cubby is a Progressive Web App — it works in any browser on any phone or tablet. You can add it to your home screen in two taps, but you never have to visit an app store.

Does Cubby have ads or sell data?

No. Cubby has no ads, no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel and no third-party tracking. Your family's health data is not monetised.
