- ✅ Free, no ads, **no third-party trackers**, **never sold**.
- ✅ **Mother-owned health**: she controls, per item, who in her circle sees what (server-enforced Firestore rules, live).
- ✅ **Mood / mental-health notes can never be shared** with anyone — enforced in the rules, not a toggle.
- ✅ One app across the lifecycle: **trying → pregnancy → baby → child**.
- ✅ **170 countries**: antenatal schedules (15 verified national + WHO-aligned) + official vaccine schedules.
- ✅ Export / delete your data anytime. Early access.
- ❌ Do NOT claim end-to-end / zero-knowledge encryption or "we can't see your data" — NOT accurate (it's strict access-control + no-selling/ads, not E2E). If asked, say exactly that.
