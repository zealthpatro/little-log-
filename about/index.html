About Cubby

One calm place for the person carrying a whole little world in their head.

What Cubby is

Cubby is a warm, private baby and pregnancy tracker. You log feeds, naps, nappies, pumping and growth, follow your country's vaccine schedule with gentle reminders, and keep every memory, all shared in real time with the people who help care for your little one. It works from pregnancy through the early years, in the same app, with the same family circle.

Who it is for

For the parent who holds the thousand tiny details, the feeds, the firsts, the next appointment, and for the partner, grandparent or nanny who wants to help without having to ask. Cubby is built for the anxious, hopeful, tired and joyful reality of caring for a baby, so the details never live in one tired head.

Your data stays yours

Cubby has no ads and no third-party trackers. Your family's information is private to your circle, not sold, not mined, not handed to advertisers. That is a promise, not a setting. Read more in Why Cubby and our privacy policy.

How our health content is made

Our guides are summarised from official sources including the NHS, WHO, CDC and AAP, and written to inform, never to alarm. They are informational and not a substitute for medical advice. See our editorial standards.
