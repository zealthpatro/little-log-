🐻 What we stand for

We hold what you carry

Cubby is one quiet place for you and the people who love your child, from two lines on a test to a big kid. No guilt, no noise, nothing sold. A quiet day is allowed, and a gap is a chapter, not a failure. Here are the promises we build into the product, not just onto this page.

🔒
Private by design
Your log is locked to the people you invite, enforced by server-side rules and encrypted in transit and at rest. Privacy you can check, not just a promise. How privacy works.
🤍
Your data is yours, never sold
No ads, no third-party trackers, never sold. Cubby earns from Cubby Pro, never from you. Export or delete everything, yourself, anytime.
🤝
You own it, you share by consent
Nothing is shared until you say so. A mother's health and her pregnancy journey stay hers, shared item by item with only the people she chooses, and her mood notes can never be shared. See how.
🐻
Free for what matters, forever
Logging, caregiver sharing, your country's vaccine schedule, growth charts and the health hub stay free, always. Pro is a little extra delight, never a wall around the basics. See pricing.
📚
Built on official guidance, honest about our limits
Our schedules and articles come from official sources like the NHS, CDC, WHO and STIKO, and we tell you plainly what we do not yet have. Our editorial process.
👨‍👩‍👧
Made for the whole care circle
Parents, grandparents, the nanny, daycare. Everyone sees the same log the moment it happens, with who-did-what on every entry. See the features.
🤍 Our promises

What we will never do

Your safe space is a promise, and one we hold ourselves to, enforced in the product, not just written here.

  • Sell or rent your family's data, ever.
  • Show you ads or load third-party trackers.
  • Paywall logging or caregiver sharing. The core stays free.
  • Fabricate health information, or take paid placement in our articles.
🐻 Who we are

Made by parents, shaped by real families

Cubby is built in early access and shaped by the families using it. We are parents who wanted one calm place for the whole care circle, private by default and ours to keep, so we built it. Every note you send in the app is read by a person, and it shapes what comes next.

One app from trying and expecting to baby and big kid, so when your little one arrives there is no new app to download. The story never restarts. It is already home.
