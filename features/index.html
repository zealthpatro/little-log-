Vaccines, your doctors and every visit

Never scramble before an appointment again.

Cubby loads your country's official vaccine schedule (NHS, CDC, STIKO, India's IAP/ACVIP, WHO-aligned and more), every due date marked so none slips by, each source cited and linked. Save your doctors and clinics, keep a note of every visit, and walk in with a one-tap summary of feeds, growth, medicines and symptoms, not a foggy memory.

👨‍👩‍👧 Your care circle

Everyone who helps, on the same page.

Add your partner, a grandparent or your nanny to the circle, and choose who can do everything. Each entry shows who did it, in a warm name like Mama Bear or Nanny, updated live the moment it happens. When someone moves on, switch them off, and everything they logged stays exactly where it is.

🖼️ Photos, milestones & keepsakes

Every photo, every first, kept beautifully.

Your photos gather in one album, with a slideshow on your home screen. Mark the firsts, first tooth, first steps, first words, from a big milestone library, with a guide to what's typical for their age. Then turn a favourite photo into a polished card, a monthly recap or a birth poster, ready to share.

🌿 Rituals

A gentle rhythm for the day, never a schedule to keep.

Cubby starts you with a few age-right rituals, a morning feed, tummy time, a bath, a bedtime feed, and you can rename, retime or remove any of them. Tap one when it happens and it lands in your log, credited to whoever did it. Then "your rhythm this week" shows the last seven days as soft dots: a look back, not a target. A quiet day is always allowed, and a gap is a chapter, not a failure.

Calm by design

Private, peaceful, and yours to keep.

None of this is an accident. It's built into how Cubby works.

✕
Nothing you log is sold, and no ad follows you out.
Your record is readable only by the people you invited, and that is enforced by the database rules rather than by the screen. Cubby carries no third-party trackers, and it earns from Cubby Pro, never from you.
✕
Never a noisy nudge.
A medicine dose is the only thing we nudge you about unless you ask for more. Never a feed, never a "streak", never guilt.
✕
Never really gone.
Delete something by mistake? It waits safely in Recently deleted. And everything is yours to export or remove for good, anytime.
