⚠️ Built on official guidance

Serious about getting the medical part right

Every schedule and threshold in Cubby traces to official guidance: NHS, ACOG, WHO, NICE and the CDC's urgent maternal warning signs. Cubby never diagnoses. It shows you the common guideline, reminds you to confirm your own plan with your team, and when something needs attention it says one thing clearly: contact your midwife or doctor.

  • Urgent warning signs, one tap from the home screen
  • Sources cited and linked, reviewed against guidance yearly
  • Informational only, never a substitute for your provider

🎛 Yours to choose

Turn on only what's relevant to you

Cubby starts calm and stays that way. Glucose, blood pressure, supplements, nausea, each one is off until you switch it on, so you only see the trackers that fit your pregnancy. Need them later? Turn them on then. Done with one? Tuck it away. Nothing clutters the days you don't need it.

  • Glucose, blood pressure, supplements and nausea, each opt-in
  • Switch one on the day it matters, off when it's behind you
  • Your health stays private to you, shared only with whom you choose
