A strange and wonderful kind of waiting.

How big are they this week, was that a kick, when is the next scan. The questions run quietly underneath, and most of them live only in your head, often at 3am.

Cubby holds it for you, week by week.

So you can feel the wonder without carrying all the admin. No charts shouting, no pressure to log. One place that knows where you are and what is coming next.

Yours, and only yours

Your pregnancy is nobody's business but yours.

Your body, your data. Enforced in how the app is built, not just promised on a page.

✕
Never a family feed for your body.
Your weight, blood pressure, glucose and pregnancy live in your space, shared item by item with only the people you choose, or no one at all.
✕
Never shared mood notes.
Your mental-health notes can be seen only by you, ever. Not by us, not by a setting we could quietly change later. It is built into the lock.
✕
Never sold, never an ad.
No third-party trackers. Cubby earns from Cubby Pro, never from you. Export or delete everything, yourself, anytime.

Wherever you are

Trying, just found out, or counting weeks?

Cubby meets you where you are: light preparation while you're trying, an estimate of your weeks from a positive test, or pick up right at your due date. No pressure, ever, and a tender path if things don't go as hoped.
