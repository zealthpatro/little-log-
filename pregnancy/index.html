⚠️ Built on official guidance

Serious about getting the medical part right

Every schedule and threshold in Den traces to official guidance: NHS, ACOG, WHO, NICE and the CDC's urgent maternal warning signs. Den never diagnoses. It shows you the common guideline, reminds you to confirm your own plan with your team, and when something needs attention it says one thing clearly: contact your midwife or doctor.

  • Urgent warning signs, one tap from the home screen
  • Sources cited and linked, reviewed against guidance yearly
  • Informational only, never a substitute for your provider
